# Reproducibility scope

This package reproduces the reduced-form analyses for the article on formal and informal employment following Türkiye's 2023 EYT pension reform.

The main fixed-cohort design compares men born in 1975–1981 with men born in 1982–1984. A complementary fixed-age design compares men aged 43–49 with men aged 40–42. The package also contains pre-reform diagnostics, alternative cohort and age definitions, gender comparisons, work-arrangement decompositions, education heterogeneity, observed-composition standardization, and direct 2025-minus-2024 coefficient contrasts.

The HLFS does not observe first social insurance registration, contribution days, EYT applications, pension award dates, Social Security Support Contribution status, or individual transitions. Cohort and age indicators are therefore proxies for expected reform exposure, not observed eligibility. Causal reduced-form interpretation requires the assumptions documented in the article. Fixed-cohort and fixed-age designs estimate different parameters; their directional agreement is triangulation rather than independent replication.

Current-job-tenure experiments are deliberately excluded because current-job start year is not first insurance registration and these analyses are not part of the final article. Word, Overleaf, copyrighted reference PDFs, raw microdata, build logs, and author-only submission material are likewise excluded.
